About

I’m Alina. I work where quantitative methods meet public policy, and I’m most interested in how governments, especially middle powers, can govern AI and the compute behind it.

I’m in the second year of the MPA in Data Science for Public Policy at LSE. Alongside my studies I work as a data scientist at Nostos, a Lloyd’s managing general agent underwriting political violence, terrorism and cyber risk, where I build Python data pipelines, underwriting automation, conflict risk analytics and actuarial models.

Before LSE I studied Strategic Management and Business Analytics at McGill and spent a summer at PSP Investments. I grew up in the UAE. Long term, I want to work in tech policy and AI governance, at a frontier AI lab or a regulator.
